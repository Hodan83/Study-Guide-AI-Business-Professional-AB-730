# Module 2: Business Applications of AI

## Overview
This module explores how AI technologies are applied to solve real business problems across various industries and functions.

## Learning Objectives
- Identify appropriate AI use cases for different business scenarios
- Understand how AI creates business value
- Evaluate the potential impact of AI on business processes
- Recognize industry-specific AI applications

## Topics

- **Chatbots and Virtual Assistants**: 24/7 customer support

- **Automated Ticketing**: Intelligent routing and prioritization

## Evaluating AI Use Cases

**Key Questions to Ask**:
1. Is there sufficient data available?
2. Can the problem be clearly defined?
3. What is the expected ROI?
4. Are there ethical or regulatory considerations?
5. Do we have the necessary technical capabilities?
6. How will this impact employees and customers?

**Success Criteria**:
- Clear business objectives
- Measurable outcomes
- Stakeholder buy-in
- Available resources
- Realistic timelines
- Risk mitigation plan

## Key Takeaways

✓ AI can create value across all business functions  
✓ Choose use cases with clear ROI and measurable outcomes  
✓ Start with well-defined problems and good data  
✓ Consider both technical feasibility and business impact  
✓ Balance automation with human oversight  
✓ Think about ethical implications and customer trust
