# Module 7: Additional Resources

## Overview
This module provides curated additional resources for deepening your understanding of AI business concepts and staying current with industry developments.

## 📚 Recommended Books

### AI for Business Professionals
1. **"Prediction Machines: The Simple Economics of Artificial Intelligence"** by Ajay Agrawal, Joshua Gans, and Avi Goldfarb
   - Focus: Economic perspective on AI
   - Level: Business leaders and managers
   - Key Topics: AI strategy, decision-making, implementation

2. **"The AI Advantage: How to Put the Artificial Intelligence Revolution to Work"** by Thomas H. Davenport
   - Focus: Practical AI implementation
   - Level: All business professionals
   - Key Topics: Use cases, strategy, organizational change

3. **"Human + Machine: Reimagining Work in the Age of AI"** by Paul R. Daugherty and H. James Wilson
   - Focus: Human-AI collaboration
   - Level: Business leaders
   - Key Topics: Workforce transformation, business models

### AI Ethics and Responsibility
1. **"Weapons of Math Destruction"** by Cathy O'Neil
   - Focus: Risks and biases in algorithms
   - Level: All readers
   - Key Topics: Algorithmic bias, fairness, social impact

2. **"AI Ethics"** by Mark Coeckelbergh
   - Focus: Ethical frameworks for AI
   - Level: Intermediate
   - Key Topics: Ethics, governance, societal impact

### AI Fundamentals
1. **"Artificial Intelligence: A Guide for Thinking Humans"** by Melanie Mitchell
   - Focus: Understanding AI technology
   - Level: Non-technical readers
   - Key Topics: AI capabilities, limitations, future

2. **"The Master Algorithm"** by Pedro Domingos
   - Focus: Machine learning concepts
   - Level: Intermediate
   - Key Topics: ML approaches, algorithms, applications

## ⚠️ Resource Evaluation Tips

When using external resources:
1. **Verify Sources**: Check author credentials
2. **Check Date**: AI evolves quickly; prefer recent content
3. **Cross-Reference**: Verify information across multiple sources
4. **Practical Focus**: Prioritize business-relevant content
5. **Balance**: Mix technical understanding with business application
