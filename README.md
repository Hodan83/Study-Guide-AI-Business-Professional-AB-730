# Study Guide: AI Business Professional (AB-730)

## 📚 Overview

Welcome to the comprehensive study guide for the **AI Business Professional (AB-730)** certification exam. This repository is designed to help business professionals understand artificial intelligence concepts, applications, and best practices for implementing AI solutions in business contexts.

## 🎯 About the Exam

The AI Business Professional certification validates your knowledge and skills in:
- Understanding AI fundamentals and core concepts
- Identifying business use cases for AI
- Evaluating AI solutions and their impact
- Managing AI projects and implementations
- Understanding ethical considerations and responsible AI
- Assessing AI risks and governance
