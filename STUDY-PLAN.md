# AI Business Professional Study Plan

## 📅 Recommended Study Timeline

This study plan is designed for working professionals preparing for the AI Business Professional (AB-730) certification. Adjust the timeline based on your existing knowledge and available study time.

---

  - Review basic terminology

## 📝 Daily Study Routine

### Effective Study Session (2 hours)
1. **Review (15 min):** Previous day's content
2. **New Content (60 min):** Study new material
3. **Practice (30 min):** Answer practice questions
4. **Notes (15 min):** Summarize key points

### Study Techniques

**Active Learning:**
- Explain concepts in your own words
- Create mind maps and diagrams
- Teach concepts to someone else
- Relate to real-world examples

**Spaced Repetition:**
- Review material at increasing intervals
- Use flashcards for key terms
- Revisit challenging topics regularly

**Practice Testing:**
- Take practice quizzes frequently
- Time yourself
- Review wrong answers thoroughly
- Understand why answers are correct/incorrect

## 🎯 Weekly Checkpoints

### Self-Assessment Questions
Ask yourself each week:
1. Can I explain the key concepts without notes?
2. Can I apply concepts to business scenarios?
3. Am I completing practice questions correctly?
4. What topics need more review?
5. Am I on track with my study plan?

### Adjustment Strategy
If falling behind:
- Identify time-wasters
- Break study into smaller chunks
- Focus on high-priority topics
- Consider extending timeline

If ahead of schedule:
- Deep dive into advanced topics
- Explore additional case studies
- Help others in study groups
- Create your own practice questions

## 💡 Study Tips

### Do's ✅
- Study consistently every day
- Take regular breaks (Pomodoro technique)
- Practice with real questions
- Explain concepts to others
- Stay updated with AI news
- Join study communities
- Review mistakes thoroughly
- Stay motivated and positive

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't cram at the last minute
- Don't skip practice questions
- Don't ignore weak areas
- Don't study when exhausted
- Don't just memorize without understanding
- Don't isolate yourself
- Don't give up when challenged
